What is Metal Max?

Aluminum outside. Tough inside.

Metal Max is a panel made of two aluminum skins with a solid core. We print your design right on the white face, in full colour.

Won't rust

It's aluminum, not steel. Rain and humidity are fine.

Won't warp

Stays flat and straight, even in the heat.

Lightweight

Easy to hang on a wall, fence or post.

Full colour

Your logo, photos and colours as they are.
