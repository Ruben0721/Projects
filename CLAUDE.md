# Working notes

## Building websites
- Every website must match the company's real vibe and identity. Start from what the business actually is: its logo, colours, typefaces, its own photos and promo material, its customers and the way it talks. Then design to fit that.
- Don't let a design system's random direction or a trendy gallery look override the brand. Example: Chavez Steel Houston (`chavez-steel/`) is a blue-collar Houston steel mill, so its site uses the logo's black italic wordmark, red bar and brushed steel, plus their own "Special Offer" promo style.
- Use the business's real content. Flag anything made up (sizes, prices, hours) so it can be confirmed before launch.
