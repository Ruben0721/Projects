Who we are

A team of top-tier technical and business professionals.

Along with tangible success, we pride ourselves on the professional reputation we enjoy within the industry and the great relationships we have with our many partners.

What we do

We are focused on finding new reserves with the drill bit.

Our activity is focused in the Tertiary section onshore Texas and Louisiana. We strive to maintain an active drilling program around a prospect portfolio that balances risk and reward.

How we build a drilling program

  1. We generate prospects in-house

    Our own geoscience and engineering team works up exploration prospects along the Gulf Coast.

  2. We screen prospects from other independents

    We review prospects generated by other successful independents.

  3. We drill with real working interest

    We participate in wildcat drilling at a significant level of working interest.

The result is a portfolio that balances risk and reward, and an active drilling program year after year.

Management

Mark E. Gregg

Chief Executive Officer

Founded KiwiEnergy in 2000 after a career with The Superior Oil Company, Mobil Oil and Edge Petroleum, primarily in exploration, including several years in Indonesia and Nigeria. Over 30 years of exploration experience and numerous significant discoveries along the Gulf Coast and in Nigeria.

More about Mark

B.Sc., Geophysical Engineering, Colorado School of Mines; MBA, Bauer College of Business, University of Houston. Chairman of the Board of Directors of YES Prep Public Schools, Past Chairman of the Houston Chapter of the Society of Independent Professional Earth Scientists, and a member of SEG, AAPG, Houston Producer's Forum and IPAA.

Mark and his wife Debra are active supporters of YES Prep Public Schools, DePelchin Children's Center, Geoscientists without Borders© and the UH Bauer College of Business.

Larry Wilson

President & Chief Operating Officer

Formerly with Anadarko Petroleum in drilling and production supervisory roles in the Gulf of Mexico and Algeria, and with Transocean / R&B Falcon / Cliffs as Engineering Supervisor for turnkey drilling offshore Gulf of Mexico and inland waters.

More about Larry

Also General Manager, International Engineering Services, supporting turnkey drilling operations for client PDVSA. Began his career with Cliffs Drilling Co. in 1987 in operational and engineering roles in the Gulf of Mexico, Texas, Louisiana and Venezuela.

B.S., Petroleum Engineering, Texas A&M University. Member of SPE and the American Association of Drilling Engineers (AADE).

Leonard Hruzek

Chief Financial Officer

Joined Kiwi in April 2014. Began his career as an auditor in the Energy Division of Arthur Andersen & Co.'s Houston office, then held a series of roles at Mesa Petroleum Corporation, including Controller and Vice President, Marketing and Land.

More about Leonard

Previously Chief Financial Officer of Greenhill Petroleum Corporation and Controller of Consolidated Graphics, Inc., a printing firm with 70 companies in 27 states. B.B.A. in Accounting, Texas A&M University; Certified Public Accountant.

Connie C. Noble

Partners

Square Mile Energy

KiwiEnergy shares offices and resources with Square Mile Energy LLC, a successful E&P independent that operates many of our wells, founded in 1998 by Gary W. Loveless, Kerry Bonner and Mark Bloom.

The two companies teamed up in 2003 to generate and drill exploration prospects onshore Gulf Coast, leading to numerous significant discoveries.

Square Mile Energy became the highest oil producer and second-highest gas producer in Jefferson County, Texas.
